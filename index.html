Storage System

A Windows desktop app for shops and storage rooms with electronics and computer components: products, quantities, prices, total stock value, returned items, stock in / stock out and full movement history. It works in English and Arabic, and keeps working without internet.

Your data stays yours

  • You sign in with your shop's Google account. The app creates one Google Sheet, “Storage System – <your shop>”, in your own Google Drive and keeps your products and history there.
  • The app can only use the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your inventory data is never sent to the developer of the app.
